## Roster Sync Fix

Previously, if you saved your roster when you weren't logged in and then you logged in, your roster would not be synced. Now, there will be a pop-up if the cloud and local rosters differ, or it will save to the cloud if there are no records in the cloud.

## Stygian/Abyss Summaries

Clicking the summary link inside the Abyss/Stygian pages will allow you to see an assortment of teams for popular main DPS characters.
